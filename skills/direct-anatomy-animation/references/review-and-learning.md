# Review and accumulating knowledge

## Evidence ladder

Classify records as **user requirement**, **direct observation**, **technical check**, **hypothesis**, **failed attempt**, or **validated lesson**. Include artifact/version and timecode/location where available. Evidence can establish a local improvement without proving a universal rule. Retain disagreements; do not average them into false certainty.

## Review gates

| Gate | Inspect | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Unlabeled phone-size silhouettes/context | Actual reviewer identification or self-review limitation |
| Attention | First focal point, contact scale, background competition | Frames and specific focal conflicts |
| Drawing | Contours, thickness, occlusion, landmarks | Extreme poses and joint/contact crops |
| Silent causality | Setup, action, consequence, response, payoff | Understandable events and missing links |
| Acting | Listening, gaze, anticipation, contact, recovery | Timecoded examples and mechanical/repeated poses |
| Rhythm | Speech, response gaps, active holds, reveals | Actual timeline intervals and playback observations |
| Layers | Guide off/labels on; audio replacement | Configuration and checked output |
| Export | Final decode, metadata, sync, seeking | Actual commands/results or tool limitations |
| Audience response | Retention/completion/rewatches if collected | Source/sample/window; otherwise untested |

Do not give a single quality percentage without criteria/evidence. Keep the user's “about 60 percent” as a subjective assessment of one output. Do not invent a newer rating. Encoding validity does not prove animation quality; polish does not prove addictiveness.

## Experiment loop

1. Read the ledger and inspect the accepted baseline if accessible.
2. Select a hypothesis tied to a real gap. Prefer a small meaningful change set. Preserve working mechanisms/source versions.
3. Record the observable test before rendering with the experiment template.
4. Produce at the authorized scope and inspect actual output.
5. Compare equivalent frames/motion intervals. Separate observation from interpretation; identify missing evidence.
6. Retain attributed user feedback accurately. Mark review pending until received.
7. Append results; promote supported rules, keep hypotheses tentative, explain superseded rules, and update priorities.
8. Save authorized repository changes; verify revision. Hand off outputs, checks, unresolved issues, and next experiment. Mark unapplied patches unsaved.

Keep a full experiment record beside project source and a concise reusable summary in this skill's ledger. Use repository-relative paths or durable locators. Do not leave the only record in a conversation or temporary runtime. Do not include private clips or credentials.
