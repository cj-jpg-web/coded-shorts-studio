# Recorded reference study

Carried-forward notes from prior inspection of ten supplied Dr. Healthcare-style reference videos and screenshot grids. This record does not establish fresh viewing by the current agent or audio inspection. Titles are descriptive identifiers, not verified publication titles. Timecodes are approximate; reinspect originals for precise analysis.

## Visual language

The subject/contact zone usually fills much of the frame. Walls, cavities, skin, or a quiet pale-blue field support context. Dark controlled outlines, cream/pink/burgundy anatomy, selective accents, white eyes, and readable silhouettes establish hierarchy. Activity often comes from entrances, expression changes, flow, tissue changes, or transformation rather than camera movement. Some views sustain activity for 20–30 seconds.
