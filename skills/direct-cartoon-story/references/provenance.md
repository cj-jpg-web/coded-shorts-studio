# Provenance and boundaries

This is an evolved production workflow built from the supplied MIT-licensed cartoon story package, the Emu War and Marathon iterations, the later mechanics prototype, user feedback, and sampled studies of supplied reference videos. Preserve assets/LICENSE with the drawing primitives derived from that package. Original package discovery: https://github.com/mromidev/claude-animated-shorts . Its license does not license unrelated reference videos.

Retain useful flat illustrated characters in dimensional painted worlds, word-linked direction, scene lighting, and physical examples. Replace story-specific paths, required unavailable verifier/model names, fixed narration speed/duration, inherited no-caption preferences, arbitrary gain offsets, and automatic upscale claims with the actual user's brief and measured project state.

The Emu War and Marathon are functional prior experiments, not approved reference-level artwork. The stamp/convoy prototype exposed style drift and generic scenery; do not adopt it as a new approved visual identity. Current instructions and structural tests have been exercised, but the complete upgraded multi-agent workflow still requires a future end-to-end production test. Do not claim those future results exist.

Reference observations support visible composition, presentation changes, pose/contact relationships, and selected temporal order. They do not establish source software, an exact rig, precise easing, factual truth, audio quality, or audience-retention causality. Reinspect actual relevant footage during a new production.

Release readiness has separate evidence levels: instruction/validator tests establish workflow coverage and structural behavior; mechanical rig tests establish only the actions and camera conditions actually exercised; visual/auditory reviews establish only their recorded artifact, scope, and methods. None alone establishes integrated reference fidelity or reliable autonomous delivery. The upgraded workflow remains end-to-end unproven until an authorized production completes the full planning, artwork, acting/contact, audiovisual, independent-review, repair, and exact-export acceptance gates.

A subsequent adversarial audit exposed and corrected missing reference-evidence enforcement, empty independent approvals, partial-review supersession, untracked findings, unsupported repair closure, weak research-record types, and contradictory repair vocabulary. Eighteen regression tests cover the structural gates. Capture settings/range/path tests and actual 320×568@12fps full/partial smoke exports exercise the corrected metadata-driven harness. These results establish specific software behavior, not artistic performance or the authenticity of a declared review.
